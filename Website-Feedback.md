# Voltaris Energy website — feedback notes

Review of https://www.voltarisenergy.com.au (August 2026). Ordered roughly by impact.

## High priority

1. **Price mismatch** — Meta description says systems from **$22,990**; homepage packages show **from $7,000 / $8,000**. Align one source of truth and clarify what "from" includes (rebates, STC, battery-only vs full system).
2. **Rebate claims are vague** — "Save thousands," "generous federal and state rebates," "full rebate eligibility" without naming schemes, conditions, or "subject to eligibility." Tighten copy and add clear disclaimers.
3. **Lead form is too long / off-brief** — Quote form asks about aircons, LED, insulation, induction cooktops, etc. For a solar/battery brand this feels like a VEU cross-sell form and can hurt conversion. Split "solar quote" vs "other upgrades."
4. **Duplicate / weak rebate section** — "Available Battery Rebates" repeats the same sentence as the section intro.
5. **Social proof is thin** — Only two residential testimonials (first names + suburb). Add photos, system size, bill before/after, Google reviews widget, or case studies.
6. **No structured data / thin trust footer** — No obvious JSON-LD (LocalBusiness/Organization), and little visible ABN, address, email, or licence numbers. Add proper business identity for SEO and trust.

## Content & messaging

7. **Copy sounds AI-padded** — Many sentences end with "today / now / always." Tighten for clarity and voice.
8. **Headline spacing quirks** — Rendered text can show joins like "withSmarter" and "Batteryfor" (likely line-break issues). Check live headings.
9. **Capitalisation inconsistency** — e.g. "Experienced installation Team."
10. **Package CTAs feel unfinished** — "Get a free quote ?" reads like a placeholder.
11. **"Australia-wide" vs Melbourne-first** — National claims need proof (local crews, partners, or response SLAs) or they feel overstated.
12. **Savings calculator trust** — Single quarterly-bill slider to precise ROI can feel magical. Show assumptions more clearly.
13. **Commercial page polish** — Awkward stacked lines need an editorial pass.
14. **Privacy link in consent** — Points to homepage URL, not a dedicated privacy policy page.

## UX / conversion

15. **Too many competing CTAs** — Give each section one primary action.
16. **Phone is strong — make it persistent** — Ensure sticky mobile click-to-call for 0430 741 441.
17. **Partner logos without context** — Add "installed & supported by Voltaris."
18. **FAQ is short** — Add cost after rebates, phase compatibility, existing solar, backup limits, quote turnaround.
19. **Only two package cards** — Add a third tier or a "custom system" path.

## Technical / accessibility / SEO

20. **Title vs H1 mismatch** — Align primary message.
21. **Canonical is non-www** while marketing uses www — Keep a single preferred host and redirect the other.
22. **Many images with empty alt** — Product/hero images should have meaningful alt text.
23. **No skip-to-content link** — Accessibility miss for keyboard users.
24. **Confirm analytics** — Verify GA4/GTM/ads pixels are firing.
25. **No blog/guides** — Rebate and "solar vs battery" content would help SEO.

## Brand / design

26. **Strong visual system** — Yellow CTA, navy, cream, pill nav work well; keep that discipline.
27. **Watch cream + muted grey contrast** — Some secondary text may fail WCAG on cream.
28. **Motion** — Ensure prefers-reduced-motion is respected.

## Biggest wins

Reconcile pricing, shorten the quote form to solar/battery, strengthen trust (reviews + business details), and clean rebate/disclaimer copy.
