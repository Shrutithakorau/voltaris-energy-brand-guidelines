01 — Brand overview

Who we are

Voltaris Energy is an Australian-owned, CEC-accredited solar and battery installer. We design and install premium home and commercial solar battery systems for Australian families and businesses — with honest local support from consultation through ongoing aftercare.

Mission

Help Australians cut rising power costs, store solar energy, gain backup protection during outages, and enjoy lasting daily savings through intelligent energy systems.

Positioning

Premium, trustworthy, and practical — not hype-driven. Emphasise expertise, Tier-1 hardware, rebate guidance, and end-to-end project management.

02 — Personality

How the brand feels

Trustworthy

CEC accreditation, warranties, clear process, no-pressure consultation.

Local & Australian

Australian-owned, Melbourne home base, Australia-wide coverage.

Intelligent

Tailored systems, usage-based design, savings estimates.

Premium but accessible

Tier-1 brands with clear pricing, rebate and finance pathways.

Tone of voice

Confident, clear, and helpful. Speak to homeowners and business owners in plain English. Prefer benefits and outcomes (lower bills, backup, independence) over jargon. Be specific where possible. Avoid aggressive sales language and empty buzzwords. Use Australian English.

03 — Logo

Logo & brand mark

Use official logo files only (SVG preferred). Recommended nav height ~44px. Keep clear space roughly equal to the height of the mark’s “V”. Place on white or cream.

Do

  • Maintain aspect ratio
  • Use on high-contrast backgrounds
  • Prefer SVG for digital

Don’t

  • Stretch, skew, or recolour arbitrarily
  • Add glow, outlines, or heavy shadows
  • Place on busy photos without a scrim

Usage rules

  1. Yellow (#FFC70A) is the action colour — reserve for CTAs and critical highlights.
  2. CTA text on yellow is always #090909, never white.
  3. Body text defaults to #1A1E1B on white/cream.
  4. Use navy gradients for hero depth — not purple or generic tech gradients.
  5. Lime is optional emphasis only; do not replace CTA yellow.
  6. Maintain WCAG AA contrast, especially muted greys on cream.

06 — Imagery

Photography & icons

Photography

Australian homes with solar/battery installs, clean rooftops, hybrid inverters, wall batteries. Natural daylight. Suburban/coastal Victoria feel. Avoid heavy filters and neon overlays.

Icons

Custom SVG, consistent stroke. Trust badges (CEC, Australian-owned, rebates, premium batteries). Benefit icons for savings, backup, independence, grid reduction.

Partner brands

Sungrow · Tesla · AlphaESS · Sigenergy · GoodWe · Fox Ess — use official marks only; never alter or claim manufacture.

11 — Application checklist

Before you publish

  • Headlines use approved serif/sans pairing
  • Spelling is Australian English
  • Claims match current offers, warranties, and rebate rules
  • Partner brand logos follow their guidelines
  • Contact phone / URL are correct
  • Contrast is readable on cream and navy backgrounds
